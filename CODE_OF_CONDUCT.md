# Code of conduct

We work as a research team across institutions, disciplines, and languages. We expect everyone who
contributes to these repositories (code, issues, reviews, discussions) to:

- Be respectful and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Credit others' work, data, and ideas accurately, including in commits and papers.
- Welcome questions from people new to modeling, programming, or the clinical domain.
- Report concerns privately to the organization owners listed in SUPPORT.md.

Harassment or discrimination of any kind is not tolerated. Organization owners may remove content or
restrict access to address violations. Stanford University policies also apply to Stanford personnel.
